## Assessment

**Verdict from the pipeline: discard.** The three categories are simply how the tooling
works — plug-ins bundle, skills fire workflows, MCP servers connect systems — which is
mechanics, not insight.

On coverage: `frontend-design` is already installed; `claude-seo` and
`social-media-skills` map onto `seo-audit`, `ai-seo`, `social` and `content-strategy`,
all installed. `superpowers` is project-local to `005-agency` (see
[../06-health/installed-skills-check.md](../06-health/installed-skills-check.md)).

Both the download and the CTA are gated behind "comment LIST", so the substance is the
part being withheld. Star counts are the creator's and are unverified.

## The one thing worth keeping

`caveman` — a skill whose entire job is **reducing token cost by changing register**. Not
a capability, an efficiency. There is no local equivalent and the idea is cheap to test.
Everything else is either installed or unverifiable.
