## Assessment

**Verdict from the pipeline: park.** As a shopping list it is mostly duplicate — the
entire marketing and social departments already exist locally (`seo-audit`, `ai-seo`,
`ad-creative`, `cro`, `programmatic-seo`, `marketing-psychology`, `copywriting`, `social`,
`content-strategy`, `video`, `emails`). Design is covered by `frontend-design`,
`impeccable`, `web-design-pro` and convenium-design's own set.

## What it is actually good for

**A coverage check, not an acquisition list.** Laying your ~97 skills against a
seven-department grid exposes which departments are thin. Doing that here surfaces one
honest question: **finance and legal have no local coverage at all.** That is either a
real gap or correctly out of scope — but it should be a decision, not an accident.

Note the structural echo with [`src-0005`](../04-ui-references/system-map-ui.md): same
seven-department mechanism, different creator, different month. The taxonomy is
convergent; treat "7 departments" as a genre convention rather than a discovery.
