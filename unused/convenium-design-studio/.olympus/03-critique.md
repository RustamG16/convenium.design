# Independent concept critique — Convenium.Design Studio Desktop

Scores reflect the approved brief, the existing Node/vanilla application audit, and unverified media rights. No user research, analytics baseline, or rights documentation was supplied.

| Concept | Design /40 | Usability /30 | Creativity /20 | Content /10 | Total | Delivery risk |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Convenium.Design Workbench | 35 | 29 | 15 | 9 | **88** | Low |
| Olympus Observatory | 33 | 21 | 19 | 8 | 81 | Medium/high |
| Artifact Archive | 34 | 24 | 17 | 8 | 83 | Medium |

## Convenium.Design Workbench

- Strongest advantage: the project/chat-first hierarchy directly matches the primary task and keeps the current product’s functional API surface useful during migration.
- Weakest assumption: users will recognize the brand with restrained, contextual character media rather than an ever-present visual world.
- Possible fatal risk: none identified if the shell remains keyboard-accessible and media-free fallback states are treated as first class.
- Rationale/confidence: high confidence in usability and delivery fit; medium confidence in differentiation until approved brand assets and WKO feedback are available.

## Olympus Observatory

- Strongest advantage: gives complex reusable systems a memorable visual mental model.
- Weakest assumption: that spatialization improves continuation decisions more than an accessible overview and phase list.
- Possible fatal risk: a WebGL or elaborate graph-first shell delays the core Work journey and fails performance/accessibility gates.
- Rationale/confidence: strong creative potential, but medium confidence due to missing evidence and larger migration cost.

## Artifact Archive

- Strongest advantage: supports trustworthy discovery, compatibility, and provenance across agents, skills, and systems.
- Weakest assumption: library browsing is frequent enough to warrant driving the overall product identity.
- Possible fatal risk: it can obscure the project-aware chat/composer primary action.
- Rationale/confidence: credible Library direction, but medium confidence as the global shell because existing evidence prioritizes workflow execution.

## Verdict

Convenium.Design Workbench best fits the required primary journey, incremental technical path, accessibility/performance gates, and uncertainty around media rights. It is recommended for Gate B.
