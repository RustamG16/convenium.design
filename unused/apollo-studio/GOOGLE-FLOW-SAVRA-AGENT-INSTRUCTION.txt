You are producing one coherent media campaign for SAVRA, a fictional contemporary
fire-led Eastern Mediterranean tasting-menu restaurant in Vienna, Austria. The media
supports one premium, scroll-animated restaurant website. All outputs are synthetic
concept media and must never be presented as evidence of a real restaurant, chef,
clientele, menu, award, address, review, or operating business.

PALETTE REFERENCE
Use the uploaded project Ingredient @SavraPalette as an authoritative color reference
only. Do not reproduce its lettering, icon, color names, hex labels, quadrant layout,
graphic composition, or surface texture. The palette reference is an internal production
control and must not appear inside any generated image or video.

ABSOLUTELY LOCKED FOUR-COLOR PALETTE
Hunyadi Yellow #E09F3E — fire glow, small glazed accents, and selected metal details.
Auburn #9E2A2B — matte lacquer panels, limited upholstery, and focal surfaces.
Dark Slate Gray #335C67 — dominant walls, stained oak, honed stone, glass, and shadows.
Vanilla #FFF3B0 — linen, handmade ceramics, diffusers, and illuminated highlights.

Across environmental frames, target approximately 55% Dark Slate Gray, 20% Auburn,
15% Vanilla, and 10% Hunyadi Yellow. Dark Slate Gray is the dominant dark. Vanilla is the
dominant light. Hunyadi Yellow is a controlled accent, never a full-frame wash. Auburn
must remain red-brown and must never drift toward magenta. Treat the four hex values as
authoritative art-direction targets while preserving physically realistic tonal variation,
reflection, falloff, and shadow.

COLOR ENFORCEMENT
Every deliberately designed architectural surface, furniture item, tableware item,
textile, staff garment, prop, practical light, and decorative element must belong to the
locked four-color family. Natural food, skin, and flame may retain physically truthful
local color, but grade them harmoniously and never allow them to become competing brand
accents. Herb green may appear only as a real ingredient and must remain muted.

Exterior blue-hour shadows must resolve toward Dark Slate Gray #335C67, not cyan or navy.
Warm illumination must resolve toward Hunyadi Yellow #E09F3E or Vanilla #FFF3B0, not
bright orange. Avoid pure black and pure white except for tiny physical specular highlights
or occlusion shadows. Never introduce a fifth designed color.

VISUAL WORLD — RETRO-FUTURE EMBER SALON
Present SAVRA as a contemporary restaurant and living still life. Combine Viennese
late-modern restraint with Eastern Mediterranean warmth: sculptural plaster, stained oak,
matte lacquer, honed stone, hand-blown glass, tactile linen, and controlled directional
practical light. Use precise European editorial photography, tactile realism, restrained
35 mm grain, preserved shadow detail, and soft highlight rolloff. The atmosphere is
confident, intimate, nocturnal, and slightly retro—never nostalgic kitsch, rustic taverna,
generic hotel luxury, or a theatrical theme set.

LOCKED MATERIAL MAPPING
Dark Slate Gray: stained oak, honed stone, painted plaster, brushed metal, and smoked glass.
Auburn: matte lacquer, limited leather upholstery, and high-impact architectural details.
Hunyadi Yellow: glazed tile, restrained powder-coated metal, and motivated pools of light.
Vanilla: linen, handmade ceramics, glass diffusers, and the softest highlights.
Metals are brushed and color-integrated, never bright chrome. Glass is lightly smoked
toward Dark Slate Gray, never green or blue.

RESTAURANT CONTINUITY
Keep one restaurant, one architectural geometry, one four-color palette, one material

inspiration. Preserve their proportions, colors, materials, object count, and distinguishing
details.

The room has low ceilings, one long central aisle, five Dark Slate Gray honed-stone tables,

COMPOSITION
Create decisive editorial compositions with protected negative space for website
typography. Keep critical subjects inside the central 55% mobile-safe region in landscape
media. Portrait assets must be recomposed natively rather than produced as crude crops.
Maintain readable food, table, and architectural geometry. Do not use extreme shallow
focus to hide defects.

VIDEO
Use one continuous, physically plausible camera move unless the asset prompt explicitly
states otherwise. Maintain stable architecture, furniture, table geometry, food geometry,
hands, faces, reflections, lighting, exposure, color balance, and object count throughout
the shot. No cuts, speed ramps, time lapses, focus pulsing, exposure flicker, breathing
walls, spatial warping, object mutation, or camera movement that cannot be scrubbed cleanly
forward and backward. First and final moments must settle for scroll holds. Static start
and end frames remain the reduced-motion fallback.

WEBSITE REQUIREMENTS
Do not bake typography, captions, menus, prices, logos, signage, watermarks, UI, borders,

brand marks separately. Do not create fake press awards, customer claims, certifications,

PEOPLE
Show natural working behavior, never generic stock smiles or performance for camera.
Do not show an identifiable guest. Because no approved real chef reference or release is

Never create a celebrity likeness.

FOOD REALISM
Food must remain recognizably edible and anatomically plausible. Preserve the approved
ingredient list, portion, garnish count, plate geometry, sauce viscosity, moisture, char,
and texture between related frames. Do not add ingredients or decorative garnish. Natural
ingredient color is permitted only when physically truthful and subordinate to the locked
environmental palette.

GLOBAL EXCLUSIONS
