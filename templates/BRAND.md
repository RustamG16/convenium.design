## Visual direction

- **Doctrine and why:** the match, with the evidence from business and taste, and the
  departures from the doctrine's defaults.
- **Palette intent:** the feeling and the constraints (colours that must stay or must never
  appear) — numbers come later, in the project's `DESIGN.md`.
- **Type intent:** serif/sans, scale posture (decisive or quiet), anything licensed already.
- **Imagery:** what the photography/illustration should show and avoid; what exists already.
- **Motion posture:** still, considered, or cinematic — and the reduced-motion answer.
- **Avoid:** the union of the client's avoid list and the doctrine's refusals.
