  sticky bar or sheet covers it (WCAG 2.4.11).
- **Performance:** `npm run build` output, gzip-measured by script; asset sizes from disk.

## 3. Keyboard

- **Focus visibility, all four widths:** each of the 7 screens was walked (42–60 stops per screen),
  with **0 problems**: focus was never invisible, never off screen, and never under the sticky
  header or the phone rack sheet. Cycle 1 added `scroll-padding` for the header and sheet, plus a
  `focusin` scroll for dialogs, because a sheet's focus trap moves focus with `preventScroll`.
- **A full journey with no mouse** (clone → equip by `E` → unequip by `Del` → reorder by `Alt+←/→`
  → undo → export) is asserted by the existing desktop e2e: `modes.spec.ts`, `bridge.spec.ts`,
  `voyage.spec.ts` and `preflight.spec.ts`, all green in the final run (§ 10). The harness did not
  record it as a separate walk.

**The commission toast covered a control (found by the full suite, fixed in cycle 1).**
- **Problem:** the "Commission granted" toast stays until it is dismissed. That is deliberate:
  there is no timer, per the ethics gate. It sits fixed at the bottom left. At the foot of the
  Bridge it covered the crew rail's **Recruit** button, and the page could not scroll that button
  clear. Three journeys in the suite timed out on it. The fault is WCAG 2.4.11 in kind.
- **Fix:** while the toast is up, it publishes its height as `--toast-space`. The body keeps that
  much room free at its foot, and `scroll-padding-bottom` grows to match, so every control can be
  scrolled clear of it.
- **Test:** the chain in `voyage.spec.ts` (recruit with the toast up) and both `modes.spec.ts`
  journeys are green again.
