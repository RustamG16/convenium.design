- **Judgement calls in the tables:** retrofit `measure` is run by the `analyst` (as `workflows.json` says) opening `mobile-app-shell` (the retrofit prompt loads it at audit); that is a build-phase skill on a read-only agent, so PF-14 reports it as info (off-route), and PF-14 deliberately ignores the equip-time "read-only agent" warning because PF-07 judges that per stage. Tightened: the verify stage must be the independent critic and the critic must have built nothing here. All four library ships still pass.

- **Verify reality check:** `verify.py --plan` only checks sanctioned agents, registry skills, outputs and stop. Pre-flight is therefore stricter than verify (gates, design-system order, finish review, parallel limits). `npm run test:plans` also runs a tampered plan as a control: verify.py exits 1 on it, so CLEAN means something.

## 2026-10-30 — T5 artifacts in 3D and DOM (design-engineer, scene-port phase)

- **What T4 already carried:** the eleven family meshes, variants, glyphs, bone attachment and the Turntable wearing the loadout landed in the T4 commit, because T4's required screenshots (artifacts visible on the body) cannot exist without them. T5 adds the inspector, the all-skills sheet, the lab harness and the equip proof.
- **Meshes** (`scene/artifacts/families.ts`, recipes from `artifacts.json`): four variants per family chosen by an FNV-1a hash of the skill id (`variant.ts`, plus a tilt within +-12 degrees); every family x variant at most 600 triangles (unit-tested; the whole registry is checked), all parts of a family share one MeshStandard (metalness 0.6, roughness 0.35, emissive = pigment at 0.6, base colour the pigment pulled 30 % toward ash). Extras that are not that material: the projector's additive light cone (opacity 0.25) and the active artifact's halo ring (additive, pigment). The seal's chain is one instanced torus. The registry's "instanced when > 4 visible" is not implemented: a worn loadout is nine meshes at most; the all-skills sheet draws 101 individual objects at about 60 k triangles, fine on software GL.
- **Size:** modelled at hand size, drawn at `ARTIFACT_SCALE = 1.8` times it (pose scales 1 / 0.7 / 0.8 apply on top), or they read as specks on a 2 m body. One constant.
- **Attachment:** `createPortal(mesh, bone)` per artifact on the SkeletonUtils clone. The rig's Armature is scaled 0.01 and the clone is scaled again to height, so each mount cancels the bone's world scale. Measured on all seven GLBs (bone names Head, LeftHand, RightHand, Spine02, Hips verified in every file): Hips, Spine02 and Head are axis-aligned at rest, so registry offsets work as written; the hands' Y runs down the fingers, so the hands carry the inverse of their rest rotation (averaged, within 0.3 rad across the seven) to keep the artifact upright. Tuned by eye, in `BONE_TUNE`: hand and off-hand offset `[0, -0.04, 0.02]`, head `[0, 0.16, -0.03]` (the registry value put the artifact over the face), back `[0, 0.1, -0.02]`, belt `[0, 0, 0.02]`, all scaled by height / 1.8 m. Orbit artifacts circle a group at the model root at `heightM + 0.1`, radius 0.45, 0.25 rps, tilted 0.9 rad toward the viewer; they do not use the Armature bone (static in the loop; its 0.01 scale would only add a fix). The active artifact turns on its own axis at 0.25 rps and carries a halo (reading of "orbits at 0.25 rps with the family rim": the orbit slots turn at that rate, the active artifact turns in place wherever it sits).
- **Stand-in:** fixed offsets on the capsule (`standInOffset`), visible on the fleet ship's gauge in `t4-board-fleet-gauge-1440.png`.
- **Inspector:** `ArtifactInspector` (one artifact, own canvas, slow turntable, demand frameloop under reduced motion) is what the T7 Armory mounts; the registry's preferred drei `View` single-context variant is left to T7 because the Armory grid decides it. `ArtifactSheet` draws a list in one canvas.
- **Lab harness:** `#/lab` (not in the nav, no stub text): all 101 skills through `ArtifactMesh`, the inspector, and an equip proof on a cloned ship in the real store (clone SS Full Run, pick a member and a skill, Equip runs `equip()` for the reason and dispatches `loadout/equip`; a duplicate is refused with its reason; Unequip takes it off). It creates a custom ship called "Lab ship" in the visitor's drydock; "Delete the lab ship" removes it.
- **Evidence:** `artifacts.spec.ts` (software WebGL): the sheet holds all 101 skill ids in its scene graph, 101 glyphs in the DOM across 11 families, the canvas is not blank; the inspector follows the pick; equipping `gsap-core` on the strategist adds it to the turntable's scene graph (and the picture changes), a duplicate is refused, unequip removes it; without WebGL the glyph list and the DOM equip still work. Screens: `t5-all-artifacts.png`, `t5-inspector.png`, `t5-equip-on-body.png`.
