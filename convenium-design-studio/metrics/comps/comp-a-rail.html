Eight decisions

Each one changes what the run does. The third column is what changes if you switch it.

CraftHephaestus
Conventional, familiar patterns; the safest and the least distinctive.
DirectionCalliope
One direction built from the brief's constraints and defended in writing.
EvidenceAthena
The existing page is the evidence: computed styles, real behaviour, measured defects.
StructureAthena
Information architecture first; the component choice follows it.
MotionHephaestus
Core tweens only; timeline and scroll skills join if the brief asks for them.
MediaHermes
Every asset is declared in a manifest and approved before generation.
CopyCalliope
Words are written from the brief rather than edited from what exists.
VerifyHermes
Laptop and desktop layouts, states, reduced motion and runtime health.
