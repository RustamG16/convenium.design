The Rustam Codex

An evolving map of identity, mastery, creation and contribution. The private universe of one inhabitant.

Klagenfurt · Version 1.0 · October 2026

The inhabitant

Which part of me wants this, and why?

Rustam Gurbanov, born 16 May 2000 in Baku, Azerbaijan, of Tsakhur heritage with roots in the Caucasus. Based in Klagenfurt, Austria, where he has studied, worked and built independent business projects. He has used Azerbaijani, Russian, English, German and Turkish.

A bachelor's degree in Information Technologies, then master's studies in Game Studies & Engineering at the University of Klagenfurt from 2021. Student employment and later digitalisation and quality-management engineering at Infineon Technologies, since left for independent business development.

Read as: a cultural identity that combines Caucasian heritage, European technical education, technological ambition and symbolism drawn from several civilisations. The profile marks this as an interpretation, not a fact.

These are proposed archetypal categories, not psychological diagnoses or fixed personality types.

The underlying order

An endless visual canvas connecting physics, consciousness, Jung, Theosophy, Kabbalah, world religions, myths and philosophical models.

The objective is comparison and synthesis, not collecting facts. Specialised agents representing different intellectual traditions let theories challenge or defend their perspectives.

Read as: a crucial distinction for the future knowledge system is to separate five kinds of claim. Connections between fields can be intellectually valuable without implying that they scientifically prove one another.

  1. Scientific evidence
  2. Philosophical arguments
  3. Historical teachings
  4. Subjective experiences
  5. Symbolic analogies

Eight knowledge domains, weighted by how often and how deeply they came up. The profile infers the weight from conversation, not from hours studied.

  • AI, software engineering and agents
  • Religion, mythology and philosophy
  • Physics, cosmology and reality
  • Visual design, 3D and storytelling

Major campaigns

The main story is The Integrated Self: a life where intellectual curiosity, creativity, practical independence, relationships, wellbeing and commitment to others can coexist. These are the campaigns beneath it.

  1. The master's thesis

    Automated quality assurance of gamified tutorials in a quality-management web application, using LLM-driven end-to-end testing and mutation testing. Playwright, Stryker and LLM-assisted analysis, supervised by Martin Pinzger.

  2. Convenium

    The agency brand, begun in hair and beauty and expanding into other verticals: websites, branding, marketing, AI automation, customer acquisition, field sales and business process improvement. Marcel on technical development, Sammy on sales and outreach.

  3. Arkhetos

    Also called Caelum and Oracle: a personal AI ecosystem centred on identity, self-exploration, companionship and intelligent connections. It should not become a generic chatbot or a shallow gamification product.

  4. Technical mastery

    Become exceptional at full-stack development and immersive, animation-rich, 3D web experiences.

  5. The knowledge map

    Construct a connected knowledge base comparing science, religion, mythology, psychology and metaphysics, with agents for each tradition.

  6. Boss encounters

    Thesis submission, first completed client deals, successful product launches, difficult negotiations, public releases, and other verifiable milestones.

The companion

An identity kernel. An adaptive companion. Mythological onboarding, cultural context, Jungian questions. The Nexus connection network, an Aid Network, an interconnected agent system.

A deeply personal experience, in the profile's words, that should not become a generic chatbot or a shallow gamification product.

Psychological development should not be equated with supernatural rank or spiritual superiority. More meaningful evidence of progress would be observable changes in behavior, reflection, relationships, and decision-making.
