The recurring question

Which part of me wants this, and why?

Not a slogan but a habit of attention: the origin of a desire, examined before it is obeyed. It is the thread running through the Alchemist, the Seeker, and every ambition below.

The Seeker’s discipline

A connection is not a proof

Physics, Jung, Kabbalah, Theosophy and myth can sit on the same table. They stay in separate registers, so that a beautiful analogy is never mistaken for evidence.

  1. 01

    Scientific evidence

    Measured, tested, falsifiable. The Higgs field is a field, not a mind.

  2. 02

    Philosophical argument

    Claims reasoned in the open, where each premise can be challenged.

  3. 03

    Historical teaching

    What a tradition actually said, in its own words and its own time.

  4. 04

    Subjective experience

    Dreams, meditation, altered states: real as experience, reported as such.

  5. 05

    Symbolic analogy

    A resonance worth following, clearly marked as a metaphor.
