# BUSINESS — Folio

Working notes, not a plan of record. Numbers are placeholders until beta.

## Positioning

"Tell your AI who you are. We make it a site." A personal site that is designed, not
templated, from a five-minute conversation you have in the tool you already use. The design
is decided by a design-DNA system built by a studio, not picked from a gallery.

## Who pays and why

- Job seekers and freelancers in design and engineering: a credible, fast personal site is
  table stakes; they do not want to design it.
- Creatives: they have work; they lack time and taste for web layout.
- Founders, consultants, researchers: credibility page in an hour.

## Competitors and the gap

| Product | What they do | Gap Folio fills |
|---|---|---|
| Linktree, Bento | Link cards | Not a portfolio; everyone looks the same |
| Read.cv, Polywork | Profile networks | Hosted on their brand; no design range |
| Carrd, Framer, Webflow, Squarespace | Builders | You must design; templates look like templates |
| Durable, 10Web, Wix ADI | AI site builders | Generic business sites; no personal narrative; LLM-generated layout |
| Super, Potion (Notion sites) | Notion → site | Needs Notion skill; still a template |

Folio: no design work by the user, no gallery, no same-looking sites, interview in their LLM.

## Pricing (placeholder)

- **Free** — one site on `name.folio.site`, Folio badge, `noindex` until verified, one
  rebuild per day.
- **Pro — €9/month or €79/year** — custom domain, no badge, unlimited rebuilds, media
  generation credits, OG image, contact form, analytics later.
- **Studio — later** — teams, multiple sites, shared doctrines, white label.

Unit economics target: a build costs < €0.10 in LLM and compute; hosting per site ~€0.

## Go-to-market

1. Launch the prompt as a free artefact first ("the portfolio questionnaire for ChatGPT")
   on the landing page and in LLM prompt communities; it carries the brand before the product
   does.
2. Role-by-role: engineers and designers first (they share and judge sites), then
   photographers and 3D artists (visual proof, high share rate), then founders and academics.
3. Every free site is a referral surface (badge).
4. Convenium Studio uses it as the entry product for its own clients.

## Risks

- Platform risk: an LLM vendor ships "make me a website" natively. Mitigation: the DNA
  system and the taste are the moat, not the interview.
- Taste risk: the DNA space is narrow and sites converge. Mitigation: the distinctness test
  in CI and new doctrine families each quarter.
- Abuse and impersonation. Mitigation: verification, badge, report link, takedown path.
