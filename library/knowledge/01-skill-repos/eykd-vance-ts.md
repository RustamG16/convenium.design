# eykd/vance-ts

**Installed: `octalysis-gamification` → `library/skills/01-design-direction/`.**

This is an application repo, not a skills collection. Only the one skill was taken. Its other
`.claude/skills` (DaisyUI and Tailwind design-language tools) are tied to that stack and
duplicate what `impeccable` already does.

## What it is for

It analyses motivation, not visuals. It maps the mechanics of a product to Yu-kai Chou's 8 core
drives at three depths: L1 drives, L2 player lifecycle (discovery → onboarding → scaffolding →
endgame), L3 player types. It is the audit and strategy layer under `gamified-ui`.
