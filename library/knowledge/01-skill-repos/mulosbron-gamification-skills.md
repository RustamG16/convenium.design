# mulosbron/gamification-skills

**Do not copy. There is no licence.** Only conclusions are recorded here.

The skills are for teachers: classroom games, Kahoot and Classcraft, semester-long XP arcs.
Ideas from `digital-gamification` that also apply outside the classroom, and are already in
`gamified-ui` in Convenium.Design's own words:

- Gamification is mostly motivational design. Points, badges and leaderboards on their own are
  the weakest form.
- Watch for the novelty effect. Early engagement measures novelty, not the system.
- Reveal the structure gradually. Do not show every mechanic on day one.
- Embed the target behaviour in the mechanic rather than rewarding it afterwards.

The repo's quantitative claims (a pass rate rising from 71% to 93%, a +20% score) are
**unverified**. It cites no sources.
